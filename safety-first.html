Ready before you enter.

Mandatory: approved safety boots and orange high-visibility vest before warehouse entry.

Before starting

  • Wear required PPE for your assigned area.
  • Complete training before operating equipment.
  • Keep walkways and emergency exits clear.
  • Never operate equipment without authorisation.
  • Report hazards, spills, damage and near misses promptly.

Stop and ask

If an instruction is unclear, a load is unstable, a product is damaged or you feel unsafe: stop, protect yourself and others, and contact your supervisor or trainer.
